# How hard is a typical urlpath law to prove in Bend?

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 01
Map: [bend-http v1 spec](../map.md)

## Question

Write a rough throwaway version of dot-segment removal plus one or two laws (for example: the output never contains a `..` segment; normalising twice equals normalising once) and get them through the checker. Record how long it took, where the proofs got hard and what helper lemmas were needed. This sets how ambitious the law lists in later tickets can be.
